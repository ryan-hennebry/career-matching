# Matching policy

Rank roles by the work they involve, the candidate's evidence and whether the role is still open. Search broadly around the agreed role families and judge the full job description.

## Review the work

Summarize the central work and its recurring outcomes. Show the lines that establish the scope. Confirm practical eligibility separately from interest and skill. Treat unconfirmed location, work authorisation, hours, compensation or credentials as conditions when the candidate needs an answer. An unstated salary or junior title alone does not downgrade a role.

Quote and classify relevant job description lines. A hard requirement uses mandatory wording or appears in an unqualified requirements list. A nice-to-have is preferred. A duty describes the work. Record what the candidate can do and what the employer can verify from the actual application. Portfolio work can be relevant even when it is absent from the CV. A hard requirement is met when the exact claim is supported by the CV. Record a portfolio-only or transferable match as partial and explain the gap. Ask about disputed facts before using them.

Assess the likely interview prospect as stronger, plausible or stretch. Keep confidence separate. Use central work, the hard requirements and the actual screening process. A specialist role calls for evidence of that speciality. Record stretch if a hard line is unmet, or if the candidate lacks the relevant depth. Two or more contested hard lines lower confidence. These are comparative judgements within a run. The system does not estimate interview odds.

## Confirm the listing and its age

Use the employer's posting and application link where possible. Record the date checked and the exact role. Prefer the employer copy for location and role scope when listings disagree. An aggregator copy is provisional even after its link is checked. A fetched page, successful HTTP response, search snippet, general careers page or unofficial mirror cannot establish that the employer is accepting applications.

Keep dated evidence for every known listing ID of the same seat. Use the earliest first-party publication date across those IDs. An older first-party capture can establish that the role existed by that date. A refresh, updated timestamp, aggregator date or new ATS ID does not reset its age. Check for same-seat reposts when a listing looks new. Compare employer, responsibilities, location and application path before combining different titles. Leave disputed duplicates separate and flag the uncertainty.

The default recency window is 35 days. Keep an unknown original date visible, and cap that role at Apply if time. A stale role needs a dated sign of renewed hiring or an explicit candidate override to stay visible. Distinguish live, stale, closed and unconfirmed.

## Recommend and order

| Tier | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Apply now | The employer posting has been assessed and confirmed live within the window. The candidate is eligible, wants the work and has credible evidence for the hard requirements. |
| Apply if time | A material condition needs checking. Examples include provisional copies, contested requirements, acceptable work, unknown age or a stale role with new hiring evidence. |
| Skip | The role conflicts with the candidate's constraints, lacks sufficient fit, is unconfirmed or is stale without a reason to retain it. |
| Pending or incomplete | The role has unresolved evidence or lacks the full description. Keep it out of the recommendation list. |

Sort by disposition first. Within each disposition, prefer a confirmed founder or hiring-manager first screen, then talent teams, then agencies and unknown routes. Never infer the screen from funding or company size. Next compare fit and confidence, desired work, source quality and age. A provisional record can appear in the main table at Apply if time. Clear evidence takes priority over assumptions about access.

Show up to ten main roles. Keep overflow and candidate-designated optional follow-ups in the appendix. A role can appear in two distinct weeks without action before it moves out of the main list. The candidate can revive the role. Suppress a confirmed submission, ongoing interview or dismissal for the same seat. Assess a different seat at the employer on its own.

Keep earlier outcomes as evidence. Record why a disposition changed when reassessing the same role. A rejection does not prove poor fit, and silence does not prove rejection. A role with no recommendation is a valid result.

## Explain the recommendation

Show the employer, exact title, source, when it was checked, the central work, why it fits and the main gap. Explain blocked, capped and pending coverage. Give a practical next action for the short list. Keep numerical scores from older records as legacy data.
